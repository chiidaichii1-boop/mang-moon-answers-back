## The entry

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever once asked how I'm doing.

One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to everyone who has ever stared up at it, wished on it, howled at it, and made it late-night promises.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). It is fully deterministic: the same line is written on every run.
